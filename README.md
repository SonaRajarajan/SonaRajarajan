## 👩‍💻 About Me

I'm **Sona**, a final-year Integrated M.Tech student in CSE with Business Analytics at VIT Chennai, graduating in 2027. I've spent the last few years doing something I genuinely love — building AI and data systems that solve real problems, not just clean datasets in notebooks.

I've had the chance to work across diverse domains: deploying ML models on cloud with **Infosys × AWS**, building IoT data pipelines at **GAO Tek**, and running supply chain analytics at **MIB Industries**. Along the way I picked up a **patent under review**, two hackathon wins, and a deep appreciation for systems that actually hold up under real-world conditions.

Right now I'm working on NLP-driven consumer research, logistics forecasting, and a gamified wellness platform — and actively looking for my next internship challenge.
